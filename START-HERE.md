# Start here

Five minutes to understand what you're setting up and the order to do it in.

## What you're setting up

Founders AI OS is one web app for running your business with AI. You talk to Jarvis or AI Chat, the AI Team drafts the work, and the drafts wait in the Newsfeed for your yes. Your brand, offers, content, funnels, programs, contacts and numbers live in the same place, so every answer starts from your business.

It comes trained on a set of funnel, offer, content and sales frameworks. The AI Coach answers questions the way you would, starting from your Playbook, and it never shares your private numbers or personal details.

No coding needed. You copy values between a few websites, and SETUP.md tells you exactly which ones.

## The 4 pieces

| Piece | What it does | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Your copy of this code | The app itself, in a private GitHub repository you own | Free |
| Vercel | Runs the app at your web address, plus the scheduled agents | Pro plan, $20 a month |
| Supabase | Your database, file storage and sign-in | Free to start, Pro at $25 a month when you grow |
| AI keys | Claude or OpenAI do the thinking | You pay the AI company for what you use |

## The order

1. **Try the demo.** Two minutes, on your own computer. The steps are in [README.md](README.md#look-around-first).
2. **Follow [SETUP.md](SETUP.md).** About an hour the first time. Do the steps in order.
3. **Sign up first.** The first account on a new install becomes the admin.
4. **Open Settings.** The setup check lists anything still missing, with a link to where you fix it.
5. **Add an AI key.** Systems, then Connections. Claude or OpenAI.
6. **Fill your Playbook.** Settings, then Playbook, then "Add the starter answers". Edit them so they sound like you.
7. **Fill in Brand.** Your DNA and Brain are what every agent reads first. The more you add, the better the work.
8. **Ask AI Chat for something real.** A sales page outline, a week of content, an offer review.

When you want to change the name, the look or the frameworks, read [CUSTOMIZE.md](CUSTOMIZE.md).

## Good to know

- **Keep your copy private.** Your repository holds your settings and your changes. The license allows private copies for your business and your clients, and nothing more.
- **Your data stays yours.** Everything lives in your own Supabase project, and saved keys are encrypted with a key only your server has.
- **Start with a few connections.** An AI key and the one or two tools you use every day. Add the rest when a real need shows up.

## If you get stuck

Errors in the app say what to do and link to where you fix it. [SETUP.md](SETUP.md#when-something-goes-wrong) ends with the fixes for the most common problems. Using an AI coding tool? Open this folder in it and describe what you see. [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) tells it how the app fits together.
