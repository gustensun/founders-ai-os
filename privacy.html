Founders AI OS

Privacy policy

Effective from the day this workspace went live

Founders AI OS is a command center for founders. This copy is run by the person or business that invited you, called "we" on this page. This page explains what it stores, why, and how to remove it. Questions go to the person who invited you.

What we store

  • Your account: name, email address and a password hash, held by our database provider.
  • What you add: goals, notes, Brain documents and facts, drafts, research and chat messages.
  • Connections you choose to make, such as HighLevel, YouTube, Zoom or an AI model key. Keys and tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they are saved, and only the server can open them.
  • Numbers read from those connections, such as subscriber counts, video stats, payments and contacts, so the OS can show progress and track goals.

Google and YouTube data

If you sign in with Google to connect YouTube, the OS asks for read-only access to your channel (youtube.readonly) and its analytics (yt-analytics.readonly). It reads channel statistics, your recent uploads and watch-time reports to show them in your workspace and to track goals you set. You can also connect a channel by pasting its public link, which uses only public data. Other Google connections are optional and read-only too: Calendar (upcoming events for your morning brief), Drive (documents you pick for the Brain), Analytics and Search Console (site traffic for your goals), and Google Ads (ad results).

Founders AI OS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or let people read it except with your permission, for security, or to comply with law. We do not use it to train general AI models.

The OS uses the YouTube API Services. By connecting YouTube you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google’s handling is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke access at any time from your Google security settings.

How it is used

Only to run your workspace: show your numbers, track goals, answer your questions and draft content you approve. When you connect your own OpenAI or Anthropic key, the text you send to the AI chat or Brain goes to that provider under your account.

Who processes it

  • Supabase (database and sign-in)
  • Vercel (hosting)
  • Providers you connect yourself, under their own terms

Keeping and deleting

Disconnecting a service in Connections deletes its saved credentials. Your data stays while your account is open. You can delete your account and everything in it at any time in Settings, under Danger zone. Deletion is immediate and permanent. You can also ask the person who runs this workspace and we do it within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before it applies.
